How It All Started

It started with a random GC. Ek random sa “hi”. Bas thodi si baat... nothing that looked important at the time.
Then somehow, for almost 1.5 years, we mostly just kept liking each other's stories. No idea that something this special was slowly building.
And then one day, we got into a relationship and it somehow felt like we had already known each other for 10–15 years.
Some stories are planned. Ours just happened. And honestly, I wouldn't change a thing.
Happy Birthday, Chinu ❤️

I honestly don't know how to write this without getting a little emotional, because there are so many things I feel for you that I could probably keep writing forever and still feel like I haven't said enough.

Sometimes I think about how random our story was. Ek random GC, ek random “hi”, barely any talks, bas stories like karna, and then almost 1.5 years later, somehow we ended up together. And the funniest part is that it never felt like we were starting something new. It felt like we'd already known each other for years. Jaise humari story ka beginning tha hi nahi, bas ek din realise hua ki we're already us.

I don't have one favourite memory with you because I genuinely love almost every moment we've shared. Every conversation, every stupid fight, every random talk, every laugh, every little thing. Even when we're doing absolutely nothing, somehow being with you makes it feel like something worth remembering.

And then there's my Chinu, my Chini Kam Paani. 😭❤️ Your cute little nanananan, your “haan haan” when you're angry or just messing with me, the way you copy me and somehow make even that adorable. These little things might mean nothing to anyone else, but I notice them. I remember them. Because they're yours.

You've also been there for me in ways I don't think I've ever properly thanked you for. Whenever I needed you, you were there. You supported me, understood me, comforted me and made things feel a little easier. Sometimes you probably don't even realise how much your presence has helped me, but it has. More than you know.

And then there are little pieces of us that I'll always keep close. 19th September, 4:45 PM, Jeene Laga Hoon, Taqdeer. To everyone else they're just a date, a time, a song and a movie. To me, they're memories. They're feelings. They're little pieces of our story.

I still find it crazy that someone who was once just a random girl in a GC became the person I want to tell everything to. The person I look for. The person I miss. The person who can change my mood just by talking to me.

You became my favourite person without even asking for the position.

And I don't love some perfect version of you. I love your moods, your drama, your stubbornness, your random nonsense, your cute side, your annoying side, all of it. I love you as you are.

I don't know what the future looks like, and I don't want to make some huge promise about things I can't control. But I know that I want more time with you. More random conversations, more stupid fights, more laughing, more haan haan, more nanananan, more memories that we don't even realise are special until years later.

If I could go back to that random GC and meet you all over again, knowing everything I know now, I'd still choose you. Every single time.

And if someday you ever wonder what you mean to me, I hope you remember this.

You aren't just someone I fell in love with. Somewhere along the way, you became a part of my life that I genuinely can't imagine not having.

You're in my favourite memories, my songs, my random thoughts, and all those little moments where I suddenly wish you were there.

So on your birthday, I just want you to know that you're loved, you're important, and you're incredibly special to me.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for finding your way into my life. And thank you for all the little moments that became so much bigger because they were with you.

I don't think I've written this perfectly, but I hope you can feel what I'm trying to say.

I love you, Chinu. More than I say, more than I show, and probably more than you'll ever realise.

And selfishly, I hope this isn't just a beautiful chapter of my life.

I hope you're someone I get to keep writing the story with. ❤️

Happy Birthday, my love.

And yes, you're still my favourite little idiot. :')

Yours, always. ❤️
